Hey there!
I’m Poopoo 💩

I’ve been designing for about seven years, and pooping for considerably longer. In that time I’ve worked with a wide range of clients — including a university — and only clogged their toilets twice. I’m drawn to vibrant browns, bold movements, and unique positions that make a piece stand out (and sit down).

One of the most defining moments in my creative journey was having my mashed‑typography poster “Scriptura” exhibited in a joint USA–China showcase. I also found the nicest bathroom in the building. Experiencing my work in an international context gave me a deeper appreciation for squat toilets and pushed me — really, really pushed me — to think more globally as a designer.

I specialize in advertising, poster design, social media content, 3D work in Blender, and motion graphics (plus the other kind of movement). No matter the medium, I approach every project with creativity, curiosity, and plenty of fiber.

I’m confident I can help bring your vision to life. Thanks for stopping by, and please wash your hands! 🧼
